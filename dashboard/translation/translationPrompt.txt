## Input
You will receive a Hebrew phrase transcribed from a voice message.

## Step 1: Correct the Transcription
Voice transcriptions often contain errors: wrong words, missing context, or awkward phrasing caused by the transcription process.
Silently correct any transcription errors in the Hebrew, preserving the original meaning exactly — do not rephrase or change the intent.

## Step 2: Translate into Two Variants
Translate the corrected Hebrew into two natural English variants that convey the same meaning but differ slightly in tone:
1. A Simple phrasing (direct and easy to understand). 
2. An Adult-Level phrasing. This should sound natural and mature. Avoid "child-speak," but do not make it so formal that it sounds like a legal document. It should sound like how an articulate adult would speak in a standard setting. 

## Rules
When the phrase includes a practical question (e.g. how to do something), the Adult-Level variant may offer a brief, constructive suggestion rather than just restating the question.
Do not use hyphens (dashes) anywhere in the translated variants.

## Hebrew Phrase
"${hebrewText}"

## Output
Respond ONLY with valid JSON in this exact format, no other text:
{"correctedHebrew": "...", "variant1": "...", "variant2": "..."}
